Production tooling, optical components & reliability testing

Developed manufacturable mechanical components and production tools, then checked designs against practical assembly and hardware-test requirements. 15% FASTER INSTALLATION   ·   30% FASTER ASSEMBLY   ·   LIGHT PIPES RELEASED FOR 8 PRODUCTS

01 / Precision installation tooling

  • Designed and FEA-validated a custom i-PEX PCB connector installation tool in Creo, addressing assembly force application, repeatability and operator ergonomics.

  • Translated delicate electronics-handling requirements into a practical, operator-facing mechanical fixture.

02 / Assembly systems & pneumatic tooling

  • Designed production fixtures with an emphasis on DFMA, positional repeatability, assembly access and setup time; the resulting process reduced assembly time by approximately 30%.

  • Built and evaluated prototype parts, moving from CAD to physical iteration before production use.

03 / Optical product components

  • Designed, optically simulated and released injection-moulded LED light pipes incorporated into eight Pro AV products.
  • Used Creo and TracePro to consider light extraction, RGB colour mixing, brightness distribution, space constraints and mouldable geometry.
  • Collaborated across mechanical/electronic product interfaces, including PCB fit, component positioning and thermal design constraints.

04 / Thermal modelling & physical validation

  • Built ANSYS thermal models of operating hardware and compared predicted temperatures with lab measurements to improve confidence in design decisions.
  • Reported thermal predictions within approximately 7% of measured results on the relevant validation work, while considering model assumptions and test conditions.
  • Contributed to repeatable simulation/measurement workflows, reducing dependence on unverified modelling assumptions.

05 / Structural reliability testing

  • Conducted torsional loading at multiple angles and three-point bending tests for two product designs, capturing behaviour under defined load conditions.
  • Produced corresponding ANSYS Mechanical structural models using measured/loading-related inputs; helped establish a repeatable torsion-test methodology.
  • Used comparisons between physical tests and FEA to identify local stiffness and potential failure concerns.

06 / Lab process improvement

  • Designed a UV-resin curing system to solidify liquid resin waste, improving handling safety and simplifying disposal workflow.
  • Worked on practical test and fixture changes that made routine bench tasks more consistent and easier to execute.
